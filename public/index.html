Questions

Everything you'd ask before ordering

What's your minimum order?
Screen printing has a 12-piece minimum since it requires setting up screens. Embroidery and DTG (direct-to-garment) each have a 6-piece minimum — DTG is best for orders of 6–11 pieces on black or white Bella Canvas blanks, and screen printing takes over at 12 and up.
Do you only work with businesses and teams, or can I order for myself?
Both, equally. Whether it's two hundred pieces for your company or one shirt for yourself, you get the same process — free digital proof, in-house printing, and the same quality check before it ships.
How long does an order take?
Standard turnaround runs a set number of business days after you approve your proof — we'll confirm the exact date on your quote. Rush service is available for an added fee if you're on a deadline.
What file do you need for my logo?
Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, or a high-resolution PDF) print the cleanest. Don't have one? Send whatever you've got — our in-house art team can usually clean it up or rebuild it.
I don't have a finished logo — can you help?
Yes. Our art team can design something new or clean up an existing sketch or low-res image. Ask about design fees when you submit your quote request.
Can you match our exact team or brand colors?
We print to PMS color codes when you have them. No code on hand? Send a reference — a sample garment, a hex code, even a photo — and we'll match as closely as the decoration method allows.
Do you offer bulk or team pricing?
Pricing is tiered by quantity, so the more pieces on one design, the lower the per-piece cost. Mixed sizes on the same design still count toward your quantity break.
Do you work with tax-exempt schools or nonprofits?
Yes — schools, nonprofits, and churches can order tax-exempt. Send your tax-exempt certificate along with your quote request and we'll apply it to your order.
We reorder the same design every year — do we start from scratch each time?
No — we keep your artwork, colors, and sizing on file. Reordering is usually just a quick confirmation of quantities and sizes, not a new setup.
Can I supply my own garments?
In most cases, yes — bring your own blanks and we'll quote decoration only. Let us know the fabric and brand so we can confirm it'll run on our equipment.
Local pickup or shipping?
Both. Local pickup at our shop is free, and we ship completed orders nationwide.

What's your minimum order?
Business cards have no minimum — order one or a thousand. Yard signs and banners have a small per-design minimum since each size is cut individually; ask us for the exact number when you quote.
Are your yard signs weatherproof?
Yes. Standard yard signs print on corrugated plastic with UV-resistant inks and hold up through rain, wind, and sun for a full season outdoors.
Do yard signs come with stakes?
Yes — H-stakes are included with every yard sign order unless you tell us you already have your own.
Do you install banners?
We can talk through install options when you request your quote — many customers install banners themselves, and we can point you to what you'll need either way.
